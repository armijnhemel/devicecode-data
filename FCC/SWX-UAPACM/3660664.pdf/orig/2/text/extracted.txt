Hardware Overview
The UniFi Antenna, model UMA-D, is an optional mesh 
antenna designed to work with the UniFi® AC Mesh AP, 
model UAP-AC-M. With dual-band support, the UMA-D 
expands range coverage and provides customized pattern 
shaping. It is available in single- and five-packs.
Antenna Features  Optimized for 802.11ac, the UMA-D 
offers a high efficiency directional radiation pattern with 
dual polarity.
Weather-Resistant Enclosure  Encased in UV-stabilized 
plastic, the UMA-D can be used indoors or outdoors.
Versatile Mounting  The UMA-D has a ball joint mount 
designed for precise aiming and can be mounted on a 
pole or wall.
Compatibility  The UMA-D is a connectorized antenna 
that is compatible with the UAP-AC-M.
