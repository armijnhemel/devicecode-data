Technology - Is the same. 
Bandwidth - No differences. 
Antenna - The original grant SWX-UAPACL(UAP-AC-Lite) has an integrated 3 dBi PIFA 
antenna and the new grant SWX-UAPACM(UAP-AC-M) uses two 4dBi dipole antennas for an 
external Omni. 
DFS - No differences. 
Product Differences - The only differences between the two products is output power. Output 
power for grant SWX-UAPACL has a limit of 22 dBm of combined power where grant SWX-
UAPACM(UAP-AC-M) has a limit of 22.3 dBm of combined power.
