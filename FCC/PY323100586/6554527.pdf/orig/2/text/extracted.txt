FCC regulations restrict the operation of this device to indoor use only.   
a.  The operation of this device is prohibited on oil platforms, cars, trains, boats, 
and aircraft, except that operation of this device is permitted in large aircraft 
while flying above 10,000 feet.   
b.  Operation  of  transmitters  in  the  5.925-7.125  GHz  band  is  prohibited  for 
control of or communications with unmanned aircraft systems.
