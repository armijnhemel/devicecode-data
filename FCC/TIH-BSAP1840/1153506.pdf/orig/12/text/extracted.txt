V. Wall Mounting the BSAP-1840 
Use the white plastic mounting bracket that comes with the BSAP-1840 to install the AP on a 
wall or electrical box.  To mount the BSAP-1840, follow these steps:  
1.  Screw the mounting bracket to the wall or electrical box.   
2.  If mounting to drywall, use the four screws and the four wall anchors. 
3.  If mounting to a US electrical box, use two threaded screws and insert into the holes
