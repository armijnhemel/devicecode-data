Documentation Requirements 
In order to ensure regulatory compliance, when integrating the WB45NBT into a host device, it is 
necessary to meet the documentation requirements set forth by the applicable regulatory 
agencies. The following sections (FCC, Industry Canada, and European Union) outline the 
information that may be included in the user’s guide and external labels for the host devices into 
which the WB45NBT is integrated.
