4.2.1  Configure the SSID Profiles
1
Log in to the NWA (see Section 2.2 on page 18). Click Wireless LAN > SSID. The SSID screen 
appears.
2
Click the Edit icon next to the Profile1. 
3
Rename the Profile Name and SSID as SSID01. Click Apply. 
4
Repeat Step 2 and 3 to change Profile2 and Profile3 to VoIP_SSID and Guest_SSID.
