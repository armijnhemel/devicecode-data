Aiming
1.  Aim the front of the NanoBeam towards the other end of 
the wireless link, while using the bubble level to ensure 
level alignment.
2.  Hand-tighten the Lock Ring to lock the aim.
Note: Do not use a tool to tighten the Lock Ring. 
Tighten the Lock Ring by hand only.
