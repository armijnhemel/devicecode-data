Connecting Power over Ethernet
1.  Connect the other end of the Ethernet cable from the 
device to the Ethernet port labeled POE on the  
PoE Adapter.
2.  Connect an Ethernet cable from your LAN to the Ethernet 
port labeled LAN on the PoE Adapter. 
3.  Connect the power cord to the power port on the PoE 
Adapter. Connect the other end of the power cord to a 
power outlet.
