Control Your Deco with Alexa
Alexa Integration allows you to control your Deco using Amazon’s Alexa voice service. You can control and customize your home network by giving 
simple voice commands to Alexa, such as enabling the Guest Network, turning off your Deco LEDs, and more.
Follow the steps below to use Alexa voice commands to control your Deco network. 
Tip: To know more about how to set up Alexa Integration in Alexa app, refer to https://www.tp-link.com/support/faq/1571/.
Note: This feature is currently available in English (US), English (UK), German and Japanese.
