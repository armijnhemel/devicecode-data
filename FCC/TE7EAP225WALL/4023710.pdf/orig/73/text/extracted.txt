4.5  Configure LED
You can turn on or off the LED light of the EAP.
To configure LED, go to the Management > LED ON/OFF page.
Check the box to turn on or turn off the LED light of the EAP, and click Save.
4.6  Configure Wi-Fi Control (For EAP115-Wall)
EAP115-Wall has an LED/Wi-Fi button on the front panel. With Wi-Fi Control enabled, you 
can press the button to turn on or off both of the Wi-Fi and LED at the same time.
To configure Wi-Fi Control, go to the Management > Wi-Fi Control page.
Check the box to enable Wi-Fi Control and click Save.
