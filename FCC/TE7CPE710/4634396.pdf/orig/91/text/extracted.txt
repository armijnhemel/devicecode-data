Region
Specify  the  region  where  you  use  the  device.  Available  channels  and 
maximum  Transmit  Power  will  be  determined  by  the  selected  region 
according to the local laws and regulations. 
Mode
Select the protocol standard used in the wireless network.
With a frequency band of 2.4GHz, CPE210/CPE220/WBS210 supports five 
wireless modes: 802.11b, 802.11g, 802.11n, 802.11b/g and 802.11b/g/n. 
We recommend you to set the mode as 11b/g/n mixed, and all of 802.11b, 
802.11g and 802.11n wireless stations can connect to the device.
CPE510/CPE610/WBS510  has  a  frequency  band  of  5GHz,  supporting 
802.11a, 802.11n and 802.11a/n modes. We recommend you to set the 
mode as 11a/n, allowing both 802.11a and 802.11n wireless stations to 
access the device.
