Spectrum  Analysis  can  help  you  choose  the  proper  channel/frequency.  Through  the 
spectrum analysis, learn the distribution of the radio noise and intelligently select the 
channel/frequency in low noise.
(cid:20)(cid:17)(cid:797)Click Spectrum Analysis from the drop-down list on the upper-right corner and click 
Yes on the pop-up window.
(cid:21)(cid:17)(cid:797)Click Start. Observe the curves for a period of time, and then click Stop. The relatively 
low and continuous part of the average curve indicates less radio noise. Here we take 
the figure below as an example.
Note:
(cid:797)• Only  CPE510/CPE610/WBS510  has  the  select  box  of  Frequency  Range  at  the  upper-left 
corner. Select the required range and then click Start.
(cid:22)(cid:17)(cid:797)When choosing Channel/Frequency, try to avoid the spectrum with large radio noise.
