Mounting Requirements
For safety, we recommend you to use the original screws in the package when mounting. The 
following are alternatives to mount the device on the wall: 
 •
 •
 •
(For EAP115) Use 3 screws which comply with ANSI B1.1 4# or 6# standard and are longer 
than 7.5 mm. 
(For EAP225 / EAP245 / EAP265 HD) Use 3 screws which comply with ANSI B1.1 4# standard 
and are longer than 7 mm.
(For EAP610 / EAP620 HD / EAP660 HD) Use 4 screws which comply with ANSI B1.1 4# 
standard and are longer than 15 mm.
