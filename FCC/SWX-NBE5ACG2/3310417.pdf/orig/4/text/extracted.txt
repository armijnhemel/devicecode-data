Reset Button  To reset to factory defaults, press and hold the 
Reset button for more than 10 seconds while the NanoBeam 
is already powered on. Alternatively, the NanoBeam may be 
reset remotely via a Reset button located on the bottom of the 
Gigabit PoE Adapter.
Ethernet Port  This Gigabit Ethernet port is used to connect 
the power and should be connected to the LAN and DHCP 
server. Power can be provided by any of the following:
•  Gigabit PoE Adapter (included)
•  Ubiquiti Networks TOUGHSwitch™ PoE
•  Ubiquiti Networks EdgeSwitch® or EdgeRouter™ PoE
