To whom it may concern: 
The NanoBeam 5AC (G2)/NBE-5AC-Gen2 is sold through a network of distributors to wireless 
service providers. 
These products are not marketed or sold to end users. This product requires a trained 
professional, and is installed outdoors as part of the network infrastructure of the wireless service 
provider. The NanoBeam 5AC (G2) )/NBE-5AC-Gen2 Quick Start Guide installation 
requirements clearly state the requirement for professional installation, and reminds the 
professional installer of their legal obligations under Part 15. Ubiquiti offers training programs 
for professional installers. The FCC Part 15 requirements will be followed by the installers.
