Chapter 1 Introduction 
The CT-5374 Multi-DSL WLAN Router provides wired and wireless access for 
high-bandwidth applications in the home or office. It includes four fast Ethernet 
ports and supports ADSL2/2+ and VDSL2 connections with DSLAM switching. 
ADSL2+ connections support multiple simultaneous Internet connections while 
VDSL2 connections are suitable for triple play (Video + Voice + Data) applications.   
An integrated 802.11n (draft) WLAN Access Point (AP) provides faster wireless 
connections with increased range, when compared with 802.11b and 802.11g, 
without sacrificing backwards compatibility with these older wireless standards. 
WPS (Wi-Fi Protected Setup) and Wi-Fi On/Off buttons are positioned on the front 
panel for easy wireless network setup and control.
