G2.3 Bridging 
NOTE: 
This connection type is not available on the Ethernet WAN interface. 
STEP 1:  Select the Bridging radio button and click Next. You can also enable IPv6 
by ticking the checkbox (cid:4) at the bottom of this screen. 
STEP 2:  The WAN Setup - Summary screen shows a preview of the WAN service 
you have configured. Check these settings and click Apply/Save if they 
are correct, or click Back to return to the previous screen.
