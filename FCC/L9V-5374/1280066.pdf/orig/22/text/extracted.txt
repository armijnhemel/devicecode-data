ENABLE IGMP MULTICAST PROXY 
Tick the checkbox (cid:4) to enable Internet Group Membership Protocol (IGMP) 
multicast. This protocol is used by IPv4 hosts to report their multicast group 
memberships to any neighboring multicast routers. 
ENABLE MLD MULTICAST PROXY 
This option displays when IPv6 is enabled. Tick the checkbox (cid:4) to enable Multicast 
Listener Discovery (MLD). This protocol is used by IPv6 hosts to report their 
multicast group memberships to any neighboring multicast routers.
