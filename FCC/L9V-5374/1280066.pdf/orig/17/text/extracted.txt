G2 ~ WAN Connections 
In Default Mode, the CT-5374 supports one WAN connection for each interface, up 
to a maximum of 8 connections. VLAN Mux and MSC support up to 16 connections. 
To setup a WAN connection follow these instructions. 
STEP 1:  Go to the Advanced Setup (cid:5) WAN Service screen. 
STEP 2:  Click Add to create a WAN connection. The following screen will display.
