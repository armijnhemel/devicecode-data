Chapter 4 Tutorial
Log into the Web Configurator on NWA1121-NI (A) and go to the Wireless LAN > Wireless 
Settings screen.
Set the Operation Mode to Root AP.
Select the Wireless Mode. In this example, select 802.11b/g/n. 
Select Profile1 as the SSID Profile. 
Choose the Channel you want NWA1121-NI (A) to use.
Click Apply.
