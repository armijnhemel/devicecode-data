11.3  What You Need To Know
You can find the firmware for your device at www.zyxel.com. It is a file that (usually) uses the 
system model name with a "*.bin" extension, for example "[Model #].bin". The upload process uses 
HTTP (Hypertext Transfer Protocol) and may take up to two minutes. After a successful upload, the 
system will reboot.
