  FAQ 
  Why can't I visit 192.168.110.1? 
1) Check whether NIC is configured as DHCP. 
2) Ensure the computer is connected to any of the LAN ports and the 
indicator is on; otherwise you may change the cable and have a try 
again. 
3) Re-open the browser (recommend to use Google Chrome) and 
visit 192.168.110.1 again. 
4) Change the browser. 
5) Change the cable or computer. 
If all these methods fail, please restore the factory settings (hold the 
Reset Button for 10s). 
  Why can't I access the Internet after configuration? 
1) Check whether the cable is connected as the instruction, and the 
indicator is on. 
2) Check the Internet connection type. 
a. If your ISP offers an account/password, please select PPPoE and 
enter your account/password. 
b. If your ISP offers the static IP address, subnet mask, gateway and 
DNS, please select Static IP and enter the information. 
3) If you still can not access the Internet, please contact your ISP.
