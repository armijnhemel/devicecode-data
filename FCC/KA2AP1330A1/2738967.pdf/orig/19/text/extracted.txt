Using the WPS Method 
DAP-1330 will ask you to press the WPS push button on the access 
point or router you want to connect the DAP-1330 to. You have 
120 seconds to press the button on your access point or router. 
If a connection has been successfully made, the LED on the device 
will turn solid green.
