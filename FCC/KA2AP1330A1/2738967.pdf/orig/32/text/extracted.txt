This page displays details about your wireless and network connections. To access this page, click on the Management drop 
down menu at the top of the page, and select Statistics.
Statistics
Wi-Fi:
Click  on  the  Wi-Fi  tab  to  display  the  statistics  for  the 
connection between the DAP-1330 and the uplink router. 
Extended 
Wi-Fi:
Click on the Extended Wi-Fi tab to display the statistics for 
the connection between the DAP-1330 and any connected 
clients. . 
Clear:
Click the Clear button to reset the statistics.
