Mounting the AP5 
Using the provided hardware, the AP5 can be attached to a ceiling.  
To attach the AP5 to a ceiling or wall using the mounting bracket: 
1)  Attach the mounting bracket to the wall or ceiling using the provided ceiling mounting hardware kit. 
2)  Insert the provided short screws into the bottom cover of the AP5.  
Leave enough of the screws exposed to ensure that the unit can be attached to the mounting bracket. 
If extra space is required, use the provided spacers and long screws from the T-Rail mounting hardware kit to increase the 
space between the unit and the mounting bracket. 
3)  Mount the AP5 on the mounting bracket.
