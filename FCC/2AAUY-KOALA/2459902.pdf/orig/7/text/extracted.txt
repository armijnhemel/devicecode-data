1  Before you Begin 
This  section  will  guide  you  through  the  installation  process.  Placement  of  the    AP5  is  essential  to  maximize  the  access 
point’s performance. Avoid placing the AP5 in an enclosed space such as a closet, cabinet, or stairwell. 
1.1  Considerations for Wireless Installation 
The  operating  distance  of  all  wireless  devices  cannot  be  pre-determined  due  to  a  number  of  unknown  obstacles  in  the 
environment that the device is deployed in. These could be the number, thickness, and location of walls, ceilings, or other 
objects that the AP5’s wireless signals must pass through. Here are some key guidelines to allow the AP5 to have optimal 
wireless range. 
(cid:1)  Keep the number of walls and/or ceilings between the AP5 and other network devices to a minimum. Each wall 
and/or ceiling can reduce the signal strength, resulting in lower signal strength. 
(cid:1)  Building materials makes a difference. A solid metal door and/or aluminum stubs may have a significant negative 
effect on the signal strength of the AP5. Locate your wireless devices carefully so the signal can pass through a 
drywall and/or open doorways. Materials such as glass, steel, metal, concrete, water (example: fish tanks), mirrors, file 
cabinets and/or brick can also diminish wireless signal strength. 
(cid:1) 
Interference from your other electrical devices and/or appliances that generate RF noise can also diminish the AP5’s 
signal strength.  The most common types of devices are microwaves or cordless phones.
