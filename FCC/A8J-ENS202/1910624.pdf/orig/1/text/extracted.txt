FCC ID: A8J-ENS202 
Operational Description 
This device is a LONG RANGE WIRELESS 11N OUTDOOR AP/CB, which operates 
in  2.4GHz  band;  the  maximum  data  rate  could  be  up  to  300Mbps  which  OFDM 
technique. If the signal to noise radio is too poor which could not support 300Mbps, 
the 11Mbps data rate with DSSS technique will be applied. 
The transmitter of the EUT is powered by the 24Vdc from POE.   
The EUT is used Dipole antenna with RSMA connector. 
The other instruction, please have a look at the users manual.
