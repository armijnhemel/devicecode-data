FAQ 
Q1. How to restore the CPE to its factory default 
settings?
With the CPE powered on, press and hold the RESET button on the 
CPE or the Remote Reset button on the passive PoE adapter for 
about 8 seconds until the Wireless Signal Strength LEDs flash.
