AP Detection

Current Profile 
Configure up to eight different SSIDs (four in WDS AP mode). If many client devices will be 
accessing the network, you can arrange the devices into SSID groups. Click Edit to configure the 
profile and check whether you want to enable extra SSID. 
Accept / Cancel 
Click Accept to confirm the changes or Cancel to cancel and return previous settings.
