Main/LAN  10/100 Ethernet port used to connect the power 
and should be connected to the LAN and DHCP server.
Secondary  (NanoStationM2/M3/M5 only) 10/100 Ethernet 
port used for bridging and supports software‑configurable 
PoE passthrough.
Note: In order to use PoE passthrough on the Secondary 
port, a 24V, 1A PoE adapter is required. 
Reset Button  To reset to factory defaults, press and hold the 
Reset button for more than 10 seconds while the NanoStation 
is powered on. Alternatively, a remote reset button is located 
on the bottom of the PoE Adapter.
