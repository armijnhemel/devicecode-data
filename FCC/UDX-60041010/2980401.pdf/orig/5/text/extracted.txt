2   MR52 Overview
The Meraki MR52 is an enterprise-class, dual-concurrent 4x4 MIMO 802.11ac indoor access point designed for  
         high-density deployments in offices, schools, hospitals and hotels. When connected to the Meraki Cloud Control  
         ler, the MR52 enables the creation of ultra-high speed, reliable indoor wireless networks quickly, easily and cost-   
effectively.
2.1  Package Contents 
The MR52 package contains the following: 
MR52 access point
Mounting plate
Drop ceiling mounting kit
Wall screws  
Security screws
Mounting Template
