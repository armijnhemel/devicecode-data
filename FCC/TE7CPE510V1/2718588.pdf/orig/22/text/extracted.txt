Q1.  How  to  restore  the  CPE  to  its  factory  default 
settings?
With the CPE powered on, press and hold the 'RESET' button of the 
CPE or the 'Remote Reset' button of the passive PoE adapter for about 
8 seconds until the Wireless Signal Strength LEDs flash.
