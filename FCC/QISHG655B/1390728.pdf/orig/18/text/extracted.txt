3.2 Getting Started 
To power on the HG655b, press the ON/OFF button on the rear panel of the HG655b. 
After you power on the HG655b, the DSL indicator blinks (for less than three minutes), 
which indicates that the HG655b is being activated. Later, the DSL indicator turns solid 
on. The POWER indicator is also solid on, which indicates that the HG655b is working 
properly.
