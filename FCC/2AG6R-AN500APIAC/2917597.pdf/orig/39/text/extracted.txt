Settings Menu
12.3 - Wireless Settings
The Wireless Settings screen allows configuration of the access point’s wireless settings and connections 
including 2.4GHz and 5GHz Radio settings, setup and configuration of wireless networks (SSID’s) and 
all required wireless modes, channels, security settings and, guest network configuration.
