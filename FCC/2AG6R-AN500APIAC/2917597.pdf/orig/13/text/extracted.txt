7 - Power-On and Operation
Once the access point is powered, the status LED can be used to determine proper operation.
Pro Tip – Check the wireless network connection status in your PC to see if the default SSID 
“araknis_initial” is being broadcast. If the SSID is being broadcast, you may continue to the 
next section to begin configuring device access and software setup.
7.1 - Status LED Operation
After  installing  the  access  point,  connect  the  network  and  power  cables  and  check  the  status  LED. 
Once the LED remains illuminated (no more flashes), then the device is ready to be accessed for setup.
