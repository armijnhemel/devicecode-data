2-Year Limited Warranty
Araknis Networks® products have a 2-Year Limited Warranty. This warranty includes parts and labor 
repairs on all components found to be defective in material or workmanship under normal conditions 
of use.  This warranty shall not apply to products that have been abused, modified, or disassembled. 
Products  to  be  repaired  under  this  warranty  must  be  returned  to  SnapAV  or  a  designated  service 
center with prior notification and an assigned return authorization number (RA).
