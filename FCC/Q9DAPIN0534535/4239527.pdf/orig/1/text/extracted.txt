Hewlett Packard Enterprise (Aruba Networks), declare that the conducted power is calculated 
based on a maximum EIRP value coded into the software (not changeable by the user) and the 
antenna configured by the installer (or a fixed value for the internal antenna variant). 
The maximum allowed EIRP value is derived from the results using the antenna with the lowest 
gain, which yields the highest conducted power. 
For antennas with higher gain, the conducted power is adjusted downward to ensure that the 
maximum allowed EIRP is not exceeded.
